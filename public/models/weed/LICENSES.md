The four `plant-*.glb` stage assets are generated by
`scripts/generate-weed-stage-models.mjs` and are released with this project
under CC0 1.0. The stage modelling was informed by the CC0 reference sources
[THC-Ganja](https://www.meshy.ai/3d-models/THCGanja-v2-019ba558-b61f-74eb-83a2-d16b40d58183)
and [Hemp, Weed, Cannabis Sativa](https://blendswap.com/blend/17492); no source
geometry or textures from those references are redistributed.

The equipment progression assets `pot.glb`, `pot-fabric-grow-bag.glb`,
`pot-air-perforated.glb`, `pot-hydro-autopot.glb`, and `clip-fan.glb` are
original procedural low-poly models generated by
`scripts/generate-weed-equipment-models.mjs`. They are released with this
project under CC0 1.0 and contain no third-party source geometry or textures.
The generator defines the root/soil origins, embedded materials, tier
silhouettes, and the fan's named articulation hierarchy.

Assets are bundled as binary glTF without Draco so they load with Three.js
without an additional decoder. External Poly Pizza models are their optimized
low-poly variants; generated stage models use embedded materials and no
textures above the 1024px budget.
