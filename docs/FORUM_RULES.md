[quote]
[color=#FFD700][b]Be respectful[/b][/color]
Treat other members and staff with respect. No personal attacks, hate speech, discrimination, or harassment. Disagreement is fine; abuse is not.

[color=#FFD700][b]No spam or low-effort posts[/b][/color]
Do not post the same message repeatedly, bump threads unnecessarily, or flood the forum with one-line or off-topic posts. Use the correct board for each topic.

[color=#FFD700][b]No advertising or self-promotion[/b][/color]
Do not advertise other games, sites, services, or products unless explicitly allowed (e.g. in a designated thread or board). No unsolicited links to external sites for promotion.

[color=#FFD700][b]One account per person[/b][/color]
Use only one forum account. Do not create multiple accounts to evade bans, manipulate votes, or mislead others.
[/quote]
