Prepared 13 October 2026 for Mafia Wars (mafiawars.co.uk). Use this as the written response in any Stripe dispute raised on the payments below.

## Summary for the card issuer

The cardholder bought digital in-game items from Mafia Wars. Every purchase was delivered instantly to the account that paid, and every item was used. Before any purchase was made, the cardholder accepted the game rules. Those rules say that chargeback-linked assets will be reversed and that profiting from cheating is not allowed. The cardholder later broke those rules by selling in-game points to other players for real money, and was banned for it. A chargeback now would let them keep the money they made reselling our product and also get back the money they paid us for it.

## Rules the cardholder accepted

Every account had to tick "I have read and agree to follow the game rules" before it could play. Each account's acceptance time is recorded in the accounts table above. Highlights accepted on 28 March 2026, four days before its first completed purchase. The rules shown on that screen include:

- "Fraudulent or chargeback-linked assets can be removed, including indirect transfers (e.g. Quick Trade)."
- "If we determine activity is fraudulent or abusive (including chargebacks), we reserve the right to reverse related game effects."
- "No account resale or selling 'boosting' services. Any attempt to profit from cheating is prohibited."
- "No laundering of fraudulent points/cash/items through transfers, markets, or family systems."
- "One person, one account (unless staff gave written approval)."
- "No ban evasion by using another account, device, VPN/proxy setup, or third party."
- "Breaking rules may result in warnings, reversals, suspension, or permanent ban."

## How the rules were broken

- **Selling points for real money.** On 11 October 2026 the Wanker account was caught selling in-game points to other players for real money. Our server records show its points and cash being sent to other players. The ban was posted publicly on the game forum with a screenshot as proof.
- **Moving points between players.** Highlights sent 1.65 million points to other players, including 1.33 million to one account.
- **Several accounts.** The cardholder ran at least seven accounts, against the one-person-one-account rule.
- **Ban evasion.** After the ban, the cardholder made a new account, eddy, on the same email the next evening.

## Why the chargeback should be declined

1. **The goods were delivered.** Every payment was credited to the paying account within two minutes, with server records of the balance before and after.
2. **The goods were used.** Every point bought was spent in the game. There is nothing left to return.
3. **The cardholder made every payment.** The purchases came from one account over five months (April to September 2026), at amounts the player chose. Repeat buying like this is not what card theft looks like.
4. **The terms were accepted first.** The cardholder agreed to the rules before buying. Those rules cover reversing chargeback-linked assets and ban profiting from the game.
5. **The account was closed for the cardholder's own rule breaking, not for anything we did.** The ban was for reselling our digital product for real money. Refunding them would reward that.
