[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:compass: FINDING YOUR WAY[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#FFD700][b]Menus[/b][/color]
Use the sidebar (or mobile menu) to open areas like [b]Money[/b], [b]Kill[/b], [b]Crime[/b], [b]Game[/b], [b]Casino[/b], [b]Account[/b], and [b]Social[/b]. New players: start from your [b]Dashboard[/b] or profile to see links and status at a glance.

[color=#FFD700][b]Cash vs bank[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Cash on hand[/b] — what you carry. Loses on death in most cases; some actions spend it.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Bank / Swiss[/b] — safer storage; use the bank screens to deposit and withdraw. Exact rules and caps are in [b]FAQs[/b] under money and combat.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Health[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Your health affects how hard you are to kill. It refills slowly over time while you are alive, or you can buy an instant restore from the [b]Game → Store[/b] — see [b]FAQs[/b] for timing and prices.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Bullets[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Attacks and some training use [b]bullets[/b]. Open [b]Kill → Attack[/b] to see what a hit will cost before you commit. Stock up from the [b]Store[/b] and from crimes and other earners ([b]FAQs[/b]).[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Wealth rank[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]How much [b]cash you carry[/b] can change your [b]wealth rank[/b] (shown in places like the bank). That can affect fees and perks — exact tiers are in [b]FAQs[/b] under money.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]New accounts[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Use [b]Register[/b] and [b]Login[/b] from the site home. If email verification is required, check your inbox and follow the link before playing.[/color]
[*][color=#888888]If the game sends you to [b]rules acceptance[/b], read and confirm so your account is cleared to play. Then open [b]Account → Dashboard[/b] to see shortcuts and status.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Short links[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Old bookmarks like [b]/attack[/b], [b]/bank[/b], [b]/forum[/b], [b]/travel[/b], [b]/jail[/b], and similar still redirect to the correct full pages in the menu.[/color]
[/list]
[/quote]

[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:dollar: MONEY & BUSINESSES[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#FFD700][b]Bank[/b][/color]
Open [b]Money → Bank[/b]. Deposit cash to protect it, withdraw when you need spending money. Read [b]FAQs[/b] for interest, fees, and wealth rank effects.

[color=#FFD700][b]Illegal businesses & collecting[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Open [b]Money[/b] and your illegal business pages (for example [b]Distillery[/b] if you own it).[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Run the operation, then use [b]Collect[/b] when ready to pull earnings and stock into your account.[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Timers and cooldowns show on the page — wait until the game says you can collect again.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Distillery (quick tour)[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Upgrades[/b] — improve gear and tracks over time; follow the on-page order and costs.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Maintenance[/b] — keep it healthy; neglected maintenance can hurt output or cause setbacks (see in-game warnings).[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Heat / risk[/b] — high pressure can trigger bad outcomes; use on-page actions carefully and read tooltips.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Aging cellar[/b] — queue batches, wait for ready times, then [b]Claim[/b] when the game allows.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Auto-sell[/b] — turn it on, set [b]min inventory[/b] and [b]batch size[/b], hire [b]Sales[/b] workers. It runs when you [b]Collect[/b], not in the background by itself. Money from those sales lands with your collect. Use the small help icon on the Distillery page for a plain-English summary.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Property[/b][/color]
Open [b]Money → Property[/b] (or [b]My Properties[/b]) to buy, upgrade, and collect from buildings. The UI shows income timing and actions.

[color=#FFD700][b]Quick Trade[/b][/color]
Open [b]Money → Quick Trade[/b] to list or buy token trades. Pick what you are selling or buying, enter amounts and price, and confirm. Minimums and currency types (points vs cash) are shown on the form.

[color=#FFD700][b]Lottery[/b][/color]
[b]Money → Lottery[/b] — buy tickets or entries as the page describes; draws and prizes are shown in-game.

[color=#FFD700][b]Loot Box[/b][/color]
[b]Money → Loot Box[/b] — open boxes you own or buy spins when the UI allows. Drops can include weapons, cars, and consumables; rarity and pools are summarized on the page and in [b]FAQs[/b].

[color=#FFD700][b]My Properties[/b][/color]
Use [b]My Properties[/b] (from the travel / property flow in the menu) to jump straight to buildings you already own for collect and upgrade actions, without browsing the full property market.

[color=#FFD700][b]Other money pages[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Stock Market[/b] — buy and sell shares from [b]Money → Stock Market[/b]; watch prices and fees on the page.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Racket[/b], [b]Booze Run[/b], [b]Crack the Safe[/b] — each has its own timer and risk line; read the buttons and warnings before you commit.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Other illegal businesses[/b] — if you unlock more than the Distillery, they live under [b]Money[/b] the same way: run, wait, [b]Collect[/b], repeat.[/color]
[/list]
[/quote]

[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:red_car: CARS & GARAGE[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#FFD700][b]Garage[/b][/color]
[b]Cars → Garage[/b] lists what you own. From there you can set favourites and use list actions (sell, scrap, melt for bullets, etc., depending on the car).

[color=#FFD700][b]Car details[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Click a car in the garage (or use a [b]View[/b] / profile link when the UI offers it) to open its full page: stats, art, travel time, and any extra buttons that do not fit on the row.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Buy & sell[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Cars → Buy Cars[/b] — browse tiers, pay attention to price and rarity.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Cars → Sell Cars[/b] — list or offload vehicles you do not want.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Travel[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Some screens show [b]travel time[/b] for a car when you move cities — faster cars can shorten trips. Compare in the garage or travel UI.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#888888]Values for sell, melt, and scrap can change with balance updates — check [b]Update Log[/b] when patch notes mention cars.[/color]
[/quote]

[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:gun: COMBAT & LOADOUT[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#FFD700][b]Attacking[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Open [b]Kill → Attack[/b] (or the attack flow from your menu).[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Pick a target in [b]your city[/b] (travel first if you need to).[/color]
[*][color=#888888]The game shows bullets needed, success odds, and gear — adjust weapon and armour on the armoury pages first if you are underpowered.[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Confirm the attack. Failed hits still use bullets. Full combat math is in [b]FAQs[/b].[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Weapons & armour[/b][/color]
Open [b]Kill → Armoury[/b] (or weapons/armour as labeled). Buy tiers in order, equip the best you own. Shooting range trains [b]weapon mastery[/b] per weapon.

[color=#FFD700][b]Hitlist[/b][/color]
Open [b]Kill → Hitlist[/b] to place or view bounties. Set reward, optional anonymity, and collect when you score the kill per game rules ([b]FAQs[/b]).

[color=#FFD700][b]Bodyguards[/b][/color]
Open [b]Kill → Bodyguards[/b]. Unlock slots, hire robots or invite human players, set pay where the form asks. Guards absorb damage before you in fights.

[color=#FFD700][b]Hitman for Hire[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Open [b]Kill → Hitman for Hire[/b] (when available).[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Enter a username and [b]Check[/b] — the page tells you if a contract is allowed (robot visible guard, 2+ bodyguards, no protection/cooldown).[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Pick a contractor tier and [b]Hire[/b]. Watch the mission play out, then see hit or miss. Rules and prices are in [b]FAQs → COMBAT → Hitman for Hire[/b].[/color]
[*][color=#888888]Optional: buy [b]anti-hitman protection[/b] on the same page so others cannot hire against you for a few days.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Attempt log[/b][/color]
[b]Kill → Attempts[/b] shows recent combat-related activity so you can see what happened.

[color=#FFD700][b]Molotovs[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]High-impact one-use items from loot boxes and events. They add a big chunk of damage on top of bullets when you use them in an attack ([b]FAQs[/b] for details).[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Shooting range (mastery)[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Open [b]Casino → Mini-Games → Shooting Range[/b] (or the same path from announcements). Pick a weapon you own, play the mini-game, and earn mastery for that weapon — better mastery saves bullets over time.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]After death: Dead > Alive & revive[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Game → Dead > Alive[/b] — if you start a [b]new[/b] character, you may be able to transfer most cash and points from an old dead account using the dead account’s password (see on-page steps and [b]FAQs[/b]).[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Revive[/b] — living accounts can sometimes revive a dead account on the same email for a large points cost; limits are in [b]FAQs[/b].[/color]
[/list]
[/quote]

[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Organised Crime[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Organised Crime[/b] from the menu — join or start crew heists when your rank and city allow it. The page walks through roles, equipment tiers, and payouts. [b]FAQs[/b] cover OC rules in depth.[/color]
[/list]
[/quote]

[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:airplane: TRAVEL & STATES[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#888888]Open [b]Game → Travel[/b] to move between cities. Pick a destination, pay attention to cost and travel time, and wait until arrival before you attack or use city-locked features. [b]Game → States[/b] gives a regional overview when you need it.[/color]
[/quote]

[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:family: FAMILIES[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#888888]Open [b]Game → Families[/b] to see the list. Click a family to open its page — roster, [b]Apply[/b], [b]Donate[/b], and rank info show when the crew allows them. If you are a leader or officer, extra management tools appear for your role. See [b]FAQs[/b] for family systems, bank rules, and politics.[/color]
[/quote]

[size=1.5][b][color=#D4AF37]:slot_machine: CASINO & MINI-GAMES[/color][/b][/size]

[quote]
[color=#FFD700][b]Casino hub[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888]Open [b]Casino[/b] for the main floor games: [b]Dice[/b], [b]Roulette[/b], [b]Blackjack[/b], [b]Slots[/b], [b]Horse Racing[/b], [b]Video Poker[/b], [b]MDG[/b] (mini dice game — name on the tile), [b]Multiplayer Blackjack[/b] (lobby, then join a table), and [b]Multiplayer Poker[/b] (lobby, then join a table). Each page shows stakes, buttons, and any in-game help.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Sports betting[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Casino → Sports Betting[/b] — when book events are open, they list here. A badge on the menu may show how many events are available. Pick a line, read odds and limits, and confirm your stake on the form.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Mini-games[/b][/color]
[list]
[*][color=#888888][b]Casino → Mini-Games[/b] hosts extra modes (when enabled), including [b]Snake[/b], [b]Battleships[/b], [b]The Getaway[/b], [b]Family Run[/b], [b]Whack-a-Copper[/b], [b]Famiglia[/b], [b]Minesweeper[/b], [b]Flappy Gangster[/b], [b]Shooting Range[/b], [b]8-Ball Pool[/b], [b]Boxing[/b], and [b]Racing[/b]. Open a tile to read that game’s rules and scoring on the page.[/color]
[*][color=#888888][b]Casino → Mini-Games → Leaderboard[/b] — compare high scores where the game tracks them.[/color]
[/list]

[color=#888888]If a multiplayer table looks stuck, leave and re-open the lobby, refresh, or ask staff — see [b]Update Log[/b] for recent fixes.[/color]
[/quote]

[list]

[list]

[color=#FFD700][b]Prestige & inventory[/b][/color]
